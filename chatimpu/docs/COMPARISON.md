# Chatimpu vs. Lokal and Way2News

How Chatimpu compares with the two leading Telugu local-news apps, and what we took from them. We copied no text, images or designs, only the ideas behind features that readers and reporters now expect.

## What they do well

| | Lokal | Way2News |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Hyperlocal news by district, written by a large network of local reporters | Short news cards (about 60 words) in many Indian languages, down to district and mandal level |
| Reporters | Paid contributors, with an in-app training programme for new reporters | Citizen and staff reporters |
| Beyond news | Classifieds: jobs, buy & sell, real estate, matrimony, obituaries, greetings. Plus a local information stream (prices, doctor and police numbers). | Short videos, WhatsApp status images, breaking alerts, bookmarks, dark mode |
| Trust | Moderated posts | Feedback and "report" on stories |

## Where Chatimpu is ahead

- **Open money trail for reporters:** a ledger-based wallet, withdrawal codes, UTR numbers, TDS certificates and PAN masking.
- **Clear editorial workflow:** sensitivity checks, human verification of allegations, duplicate auto-reject, and an audit log.
- **Corrections in public:** a dated correction note on the story itself, instead of a silent edit.

## Next ideas (not built)

4. Weather and crop advisories for farmers from IMD and the agriculture department.
5. Live election results pages for local-body polls.
